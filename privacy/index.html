Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026

Crewsa is operated by an independent sole developer based in Atlanta, Georgia, United States. This policy explains what the Crewsa iOS app collects, why, and what control you have. Questions: officialcrewsa@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number. Required. It is how you sign in and how we keep one person to one account.
  • Your name, and optionally a profile photo and your city.
  • If you are a camera operator: your hourly rate, biography, equipment list, and the photos and videos you upload as work samples. This information is public inside the app.
  • Booking details you enter: date, start time, length, the address of the shoot, what is being filmed and whose equipment is used.
  • Messages and reviews you write in the app.
  • Reports and blocks you submit, including the reason you choose and any note you add.
  • A push notification token, only if you allow notifications.

What we do not do

Crewsa contains no advertising, no analytics or attribution software, and no crash-reporting software. We do not collect an advertising identifier, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. Crewsa uses no artificial-intelligence or machine-learning service of any kind.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts the database, the login system, uploaded files and the app’s server functions.
  • Twilio — delivers your six-digit login code by SMS.
  • Stripe — processes card payments and pays operators out. Card numbers are entered into Stripe’s own payment sheet and never reach Crewsa’s servers. Operators complete identity and bank verification directly with Stripe.
  • Expo — builds the app and relays push notifications to Apple’s notification service.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — suggests addresses as you type one on the booking screen.

These companies act as our service providers and may only use the data to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your login, profile, profile photo and messages, and the photo and work samples on your listing; a camera operator’s listing is hidden and emptied down to the display name. Reviews you wrote keep their stars and words but are re-signed “Former user”. We keep records of completed, paid bookings in anonymised form, with your personal details stripped out, because payment and tax law requires us to retain transaction records.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time from inside the app: Me > Delete account. No email to us is required.
  • Edit your name, photo, city, rate, biography, equipment and work samples at any time from the Me tab.
  • Turn notification permission off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, by emailing officialcrewsa@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to have it deleted, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law.

Children

Crewsa is for adults. It is not directed at children, and you must be 18 or older to create an account. If we learn that we hold information from someone under 18, we delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security rules so that people can only read and write their own records. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
