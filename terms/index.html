2. What Crewsa is

Crewsa is a marketplace where creators book cameramen by the hour. Cameramen are independent contractors, not employees or agents of Crewsa. They set their own rates, use their own equipment (or the creator’s, if the booking says so), and decide whether to keep a booking. A cameraman can cancel a booking, and the creator is then refunded in full. Crewsa does not direct, supervise or guarantee the work.

The agreement for a shoot is between the creator and the cameraman. These terms are part of that agreement: for example, they decide who owns the footage (section 7) and how refunds work (section 4).

A cameraman’s listing can only be switched on, and booked, once Stripe has verified their payout account. You cannot book your own listing.

3. Prices, payment and Crewsa’s fee

  • The price of a booking is the cameraman’s hourly rate times the number of hours booked. The creator pays that price and nothing more. Creators pay no service fee.
  • The creator pays in the app when booking. Payments are processed by Stripe. A booking is confirmed only when the payment goes through. Until then, the cameraman does not see the booking and the creator is not charged.
  • Crewsa’s fee is 15% of the booking price, taken from the cameraman’s side. For example, on a $100 booking the creator pays $100, the cameraman gets $85 and Crewsa keeps $15. Crewsa pays Stripe’s card-processing fees out of its 15%.
  • When a booking is refunded in full, Crewsa does not keep its fee. When part of a payment is kept (for example the ASAP cancellation fee in section 4), the same 85% / 15% split applies to the part that is kept.
  • All payment for a shoot arranged on Crewsa must go through the app. Asking for or accepting payment outside the app is not allowed.
  • Cameramen are responsible for their own taxes and their own insurance. Stripe may ask cameramen for tax information and may send them tax forms where the law requires.

4. Cancellations, no-shows and refunds

Refunds go back to the card or payment method that was used to pay. Stripe sends a refund straight away; most banks show it within 5 to 10 business days.

  • Unpaid bookings. If a payment fails, or the creator leaves the payment screen without paying, the booking is not confirmed. If it is then cancelled, any unfinished payment is cancelled with Stripe. Nothing was charged, so there is nothing to refund.
  • A creator cancels a scheduled booking before its start time: full refund.
  • A creator cancels an ASAP booking (a booking for right now): because the cameraman is asked to come straight away, the creator is refunded the price minus a $25 cancellation fee. If the booking cost $25 or less, there is no refund. The cameraman gets the fee, less Crewsa’s 15%. An ASAP booking can be cancelled in the app for one hour after it was booked; after that the cameraman should already be with you, so it counts as started (see late cancellation and no-shows below).
  • Late cancellation by a creator. Once a scheduled booking’s start time has passed, the creator can no longer cancel it in the app and it is not refundable, because the cameraman has kept that time free and travelled to the shoot. The same applies to an ASAP booking once filming has started, and when the creator is not at the address when the cameraman arrives. If a shoot ends early because the creator chooses to stop, unused hours are not refunded.
  • A cameraman cancels: the creator gets a full refund automatically, whenever it happens. This includes ASAP bookings, with no fee. Cameramen who cancel often may be removed from Crewsa.
  • No-shows. If the cameraman does not turn up, or arrives too late for the shoot to go ahead, the creator gets a full refund, including on ASAP bookings. Email officialcrewsa@gmail.com within 48 hours of the start time with the booking details. We check with the cameraman and refund in full. If you already cancelled an ASAP booking yourself because nobody came, email us and we will refund the $25 fee as well. A cameraman who does not show up may be removed from Crewsa.
  • Completed and cancelled bookings cannot be cancelled or refunded in the app. Only mark a shoot complete after it has taken place. If something went wrong at a shoot that did take place, email us within 7 days. We will look into it with both of you and may refund all or part of the price where the shoot clearly did not go ahead as booked.

5. Payouts to cameramen

  • Cameramen are paid through Stripe. You set up payouts from Earnings in the app. Stripe collects and verifies your identity and bank details directly; Crewsa never sees your bank details.
  • When a creator pays, your 85% goes to your Stripe balance but is held there first. Stripe then pays it to your bank automatically after a holding period that Crewsa sets. The holding period is designed so that, for most bookings, money reaches your bank after the shoot has taken place, and so that refunds for cancellations, no-shows and disputes can come out of money that has not yet been paid out.
  • Stripe’s first payout to a new account can take longer, and Stripe may hold or delay payouts for its own verification or risk checks. Crewsa cannot release held money early.
  • If a booking is refunded (for example a cancellation or a no-show) or taken back through a chargeback, your share of the refunded amount is taken back from your Stripe balance and, if that is not enough, from your future payouts. You agree to this.

6. Chargebacks

  • If you have a problem with a charge, please email us first. We can usually fix it faster than your bank can.
  • A chargeback is when a card holder asks their bank to reverse a payment. When that happens, Stripe takes the disputed amount and a dispute fee from Crewsa. We respond to the bank with the booking records, the in-app messages and anything the cameraman sends us.
  • If the dispute is lost, or we refund it, Crewsa may recover the cameraman’s share of the disputed amount from their Stripe balance or future payouts. We will tell the cameraman before we do.
  • Filing a chargeback for a shoot that took place as booked breaks these terms. We may close the creator’s account, and the footage rights go back to the cameraman (section 7).

The cameraman may not use the booking footage in any other way, sell it, or give it to anyone else without the creator’s written permission.
