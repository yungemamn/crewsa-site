crewsa

Hire a camera operator by the hour.

crewsa connects creators who need someone to film them with camera operators who own gear. Browse operators near you, see their hourly rate, equipment and past work, and book a specific date, time and address. Pay in the app. Operators set their own rate and are paid out to their own bank account.

Availability. crewsa is available in the United States only. You must be 18 or older to use it.

For creators

  • Browse camera operators near you with real rates, equipment lists and work samples.
  • Book a date, start time, length and address, and say what you are filming.
  • Message your operator in the app and leave a review afterwards.

For camera operators

  • Set your own hourly rate and list the gear you own.
  • Take bookings, see them on your calendar, and message the creator.
  • Get paid out to your bank account through Stripe. crewsa keeps a 15% commission on completed shoots; creators pay no service fee.

Need help?

Email officialcrewsa@gmail.com. See the support page for common questions, including how to delete your account.
