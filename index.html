Crewsa

Book a cameraman by the hour.

Crewsa connects creators who need someone to film them with cameramen (camera operators) who own gear. See each cameraman’s hourly rate, equipment and past work, then book a specific date, time and address and pay in the app. Cameramen set their own rate and are paid out to their own bank account.

Free for iPhone.

Availability. Crewsa is available in the United States only. You must be 18 or older to use it.

For creators

  • Browse cameramen with their real rates, equipment lists and work samples.
  • Book a date, start time, length and address, and say what you are filming.
  • The price you see is the price you pay. Creators pay no service fee.
  • Cancel a scheduled booking before it starts and you get a full refund.
  • Message your cameraman in the app and leave a review afterwards.

For cameramen

  • Set your own hourly rate and list the gear you own.
  • Set up payouts with Stripe. Your listing goes live once Stripe has verified you.
  • Take bookings, see them on your calendar, and message the creator.
  • Crewsa keeps 15% of each booking. On a $100 booking you get $85, paid out to your bank through Stripe.

Need help?

Email officialcrewsa@gmail.com. See the support page for common questions, including how refunds work and how to delete your account.
